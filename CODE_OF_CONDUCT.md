# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and professional collaboration
space. Contributors are expected to communicate constructively, welcome
different perspectives and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than
people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Report concerns privately to a
repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community and the project.
